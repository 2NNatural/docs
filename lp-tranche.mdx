title: "LP Tranche (LT)"
description: "Royco's LP Tranche provides continuous secondary liquidity for Senior shares through an AMM pool, earning a liquidity premium plus trading fees."
---

The **LP Tranche (LT)** is a dedicated capital pool that earns yield by providing constant secondary liquidity for the senior tranche.

LT yield is calculated as follows:

> Liquidity Premium \+ ST Appreciation \+ Stablecoin Yield \+ Trading Fees − Impermanent Loss

Unlike ST and JT, LT is not deployed directly into the yield-bearing asset. Instead, it is deployed into a Balancer Pool Token in an AMM pool made up of ST tokens and a tokenized-treasury stablecoin.

LT earns yield from the assets deployed in the pool: both the underlying yield of the ST shares, as well as the rate earned by the paired stablecoin.

In this mechanism, LT gets paid a premium for providing exit liquidity to the ST. LT also gets additional sources of yield: a % of swap fees from the Balancer Pool, as well as swap premiums when ST flow imbalances the pool.
