---

Every Royco market starts from a single yield source: a lending market, a staking deposit, or tokenized RWA, and splits it into three tranches:

- **Senior Tranche (ST)**: earns yield with smart-contract-enforced downside coverage and secondary liquidity. A defined amount of Junior capital absorbs losses before Senior is ever impacted.
- **Junior Tranche (JT):** earns the highest yield by serving as first-loss capital and receiving a risk premium paid by Senior in return.
- **Liquidity Tranche (LT):** earns a liquidity premium and pool fees by providing secondary liquidity for the Senior tranche.

**Yield Distribution**

Yield flows outward from ST to both JT and LT, paying for Senior's downside coverage and liquidity based on pre-defined ratios.

#### Why Does This Matter?

**ST gets smart-contract-enforced downside coverage.** Every ST position has a quantified JT buffer defined in the protocol's contracts. This gives risk committees a concrete, verifiable loss-absorption layer. This enables allocators to evaluate yield sources they might otherwise be prevented from accessing.

**ST gets secondary liquidity.** Many RWAs are illiquid by design. The LT design now allows ST to buy/sell their RWAs positions at any time, with constant liquidity.

**JT absorbs losses from the first dollar.** There is no buffer on losses. JT absorbs any ST drawdown from the very first dollar, up to the full coverage amount. ST is not impaired until JT capital is fully exhausted. For JT, this effect is similar to a levered position.

**JT benefits from an observation period.** When the underlying strategy draws down, the protocol enters a time-bounded observation window before allocating losses. Temporary, expected volatility that reverses within the window does not slash JT capital. JT only absorbs actual, persistent losses, not noise. (Note that not all markets have an observation period. See Section 2 for details on when the observation period is triggered and how it resolves.)

**JT earns a premium for risk they already underwrite.** Every yield source Royco tranches already has LPs depositing into the underlying asset directly. Royco lets those same participants earn a risk premium for formally taking the first-loss position, rather than simply accepting the base rate. This risk premium is paid by Senior positions.

**LT gets paid to be the exit.** LT provides the counterparty for ST redemptions, earning a liquidity premium and pool fees, as well as buying shares at a discount when ST sells shares into the pool. Additionally, they still benefit from JT's first-loss coverage. In return, they accept the pool's lockup and impermanent loss in a drawdown scenario.

#### The Vault Products

For investors who do not want to evaluate and manage individual Royco markets, the protocol offers managed vaults.

| Vault | Deposit Asset | What It Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Senior Vault (srRoyUSDC) | USDC | Allocates across multiple Senior tranches for diversified, Senior-tranche yield. May utilize Senior tokens in lending protocols for levered exposure while still retaining first-loss coverage. |
| Royco ETH (roywstETH) | wstETH | Borrows USDC against wstETH collateral and deploys into srRoyUSDC, earning staking rewards plus the carry spread |

The **Senior Vault (srRoyUSDC)** is the foundational product, deploying USDC into Senior tranches across a diversified set of yield sources to offer buffered yield backed by JT first-loss capital. **Royco ETH (roywstETH)** is built for ETH holders who want to earn yield without selling their ETH: it borrows USDC against wstETH collateral and deploys the proceeds into srRoyUSDC, capturing staking rewards plus the carry spread. Each vault is managed by a professional, independent curator.
