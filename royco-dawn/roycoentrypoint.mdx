**RoycoEntryPoint** is the protocol's tranche deposit gateway contract. All deposits into Royco Dawn markets are routed through the RoycoEntryPoint rather than depositing directly into tranche contracts. This design enables several protocol-level guarantees:

1. **Queued execution:** Deposits are batched and executed in processing cycles, preventing front-running and ensuring fair ordering.
2. **Fee routing:** Protocol-level execution fees are applied at the entry point before assets are forwarded to the vault.
3. **Upgradeability:** Entry point logic can be updated by the Foundation without requiring vault contract changes.

Depositors should always interact with Royco products via the official app at royco.org, which routes transactions through the RoycoEntryPoint. Direct vault deposits bypass access controls and are not supported.
