<Warning>
  **Dawn is a legacy deployment.** Its contracts remain operational, but Dawn is no longer under active development. Users should understand the accounting behavior below before interacting with a Dawn market.
</Warning>

## Junior Tranche recovery limitation

When a Senior Tranche drawdown is absorbed by Junior Tranche capital, Dawn records the amount as Junior Tranche impermanent loss. This record gives Junior a claim on a future recovery in the Senior position while the market remains in its fixed-term recovery state.

That recovery claim is not permanent. Dawn resets the recorded Junior Tranche impermanent loss and returns the market to its perpetual state when any of the following occurs:

- The fixed-term recovery window expires before the loss is fully recovered.
- The market breaches its liquidation-utilization threshold.
- The Junior buffer is exhausted and the Senior Tranche has an uncovered impermanent loss.

After the record is reset, Junior has forfeited that recovery claim. A later recovery in the underlying position does not first restore the erased claim; subsequent gains follow the market's normal accounting and yield-distribution rules. As a result, an underlying asset returning to its pre-drawdown value does not necessarily make Junior Tranche holders whole.

Users should evaluate Dawn positions based on the deployed contracts and current onchain state, and should not assume that behavior described for Royco Day also applies to Dawn.
