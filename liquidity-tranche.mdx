title: "Liquidity Tranche (LT)"
description: "Royco's Liquidity Tranche provides continuous secondary liquidity for Senior shares through a rate-scaled Balancer pool, earning a liquidity premium plus pool fees."
---

The **Liquidity Tranche (LT)** is a dedicated capital pool that earns yield by providing constant secondary liquidity for the senior tranche.

LT yield is calculated as follows:

> Liquidity Premium \+ ST Appreciation \+ Stablecoin Yield \+ Trading Fees − Impermanent Loss

Unlike ST and JT, LT is not deployed directly into the yield-bearing asset. Instead, it is deployed into a Balancer Pool Token in an AMM pool made up of ST tokens and a tokenized-treasury stablecoin.

LT earns yield from the assets deployed in the pool: both the underlying yield of the ST shares, as well as the rate earned by the paired stablecoin.

In this mechanism, LT gets paid a premium for providing exit liquidity to the ST. LT also gets two additional sources of yield: a % of swap fees from the Balancer Pool, as well as swap premiums when ST flow imbalances the pool.

LT has the same credit profile as the ST, with the liquidity profile of the JT. Therefore, it effectively acts as a mezzanine layer with a risk profile in between that of JT and ST.
