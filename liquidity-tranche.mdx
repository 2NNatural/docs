---
title: "Liquidity Tranche"
description: "Royco's Liquidity Tranche provides continuous secondary liquidity for Senior shares through a rate-scaled Balancer pool, earning a liquidity premium plus pool fees."
---

The Liquidity Tranche (LT) is market making capital that earns yield by providing constant secondary liquidity for the senior tranche.

LT yield is calculated as follows:

$$
Liquidity Premium + ST Appreciation + Trading Fees - Impermanent Loss
$$

Unlike ST and JT, LT is not deployed directly into the yield-bearing asset. Instead, it is deployed into a Balancer Pool Token in a rate-scaled AMM pool between the ST and a stablecoin.

In this mechanism, LT gets paid a premium for providing exit liquidity to the ST. LT also gets two additional sources of yield: a % of swap fees from the Balancer Pool, as well as swap premiums when ST flow imblanaces the pool.

LT has the same underlying risk profile as the ST. However, it is illiquid and provides constant secondary liquidity for the senior tranche. Therefore, it effectively acts as a mezzanine layer with a risk profile in between that of JT and ST.
