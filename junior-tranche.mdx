---
title: "Junior Tranche"
description: "Royco's Junior tranche earns boosted yield as first-loss capital, absorbing losses before Senior and collecting a risk premium in return."
---

The Junior Tranche (JT) is first-loss capital that provides coverage to the senior tranche in the case of any losses. JT is deployed into the same yield bearing strategy as the ST.

JT yield is calculated as follows: 

$$
JT Base Yield + Senior Risk Premium - Coverage Absorbed
$$

Junior earns the highest yield of the three tranches, in exchange for the highest risk profile. The JT takes the first losses, and receives a risk premium from the ST in exchange for providing coverage.
