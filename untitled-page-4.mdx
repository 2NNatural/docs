---
title: "For Distribution"
---

**Liquid collateral for previously illiquid assets:** Senior and SLP Tranches transform duration assets into composable on-chain collateral. Coverage allows these assets an improved risk profile, justifying a higher LTV.

**Anytime Deposits:** Currently, users must wait for narrow vault deposit windows. If they miss them, this capital sits idle and can churn to competing assets. Royco enables continuous onboarding, allowing users to deploy capital into vaults at any time (in Perpetual state).

**Enable efficient looping:** Users can lever protected assets instead of the underlying, improving capital efficiency and reducing risk.
