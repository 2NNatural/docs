---
title: "Senior Tranche"
description: "Royco's Senior tranche earns protected yield backed by Junior first-loss capital, paying a risk premium for coverage and a liquidity premium for secondary liquidity."
---

The Senior Tranche (ST) is protected capital that pays the junior tranche a risk premium, and the liquidity tranche a liquidity premium.

ST yield is calculated as follows: ST Gross Yield - Risk Premium - Liquidity Premium = ST Net Yield
