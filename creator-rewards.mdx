**Overview**

The Royco Creator Rewards Program is designed to support and incentivize eligible creators who deploy new markets through Royco.

Approved creators are eligible to receive 5% of the risk and liquidity premiums generated by all markets they deploy that sustain \$20,000 TVL over a 7-day rolling period. Program terms, reward rates, eligibility requirements, and fee arrangements may be modified at any time at the Royco Foundation’s discretion.

**How to Participate**

1. Explore Royco Markets
   1. Familiarize yourself with Royco’s market structure and deployment process using the[ <u>Royco Market Simulator</u>](https://simulator.royco.org/).
2. Request Creator Access
   1. Submit an [<u>access request</u>](https://forms.gle/xgybBP6NCyhWpdj69) to join the Creator Rewards Program 
      1. If your application is approved, your deployer address will be whitelisted on the Royco app.
      2. Only markets from enrolled creators will appear on the Royco frontend.
   2. Contact support if you need help with the request process: [support@royco.org](mailto:support@royco.org)
3. Deploy a Market
   1. Once approved, use the Royco Deployer to configure and launch your markets, including its yield source, pricing, coverage, liquidity parameters, and other applicable terms.
   2. **Important: Royco markets are IMMUTABLE. If you would like to change parameters, you must deploy a new pool.**

For assistance with the application or deployment process, contact [<u>support@royco.org</u>](mailto:support@royco.org).

**Eligibility and Restrictions**

Participation is subject to the Royco Foundation’s review and approval. U.S. persons and entities are not eligible to participate.<br /><br />Deployers must have a minimum level of prior onchain activity on recognized decentralized protocols, based on transaction count and/or volume thresholds set by the Royco Foundation. Applicants may designate a separate wallet to demonstrate prior onchain activity; the wallet used to deploy Royco markets does not need to have prior history.
